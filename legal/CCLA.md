## 9. Acceptance

An authorized representative of the Corporation accepts this Agreement
by completing and signing it through Our hosted signing form (DocuSeal
Cloud), linked from https://vaelii.com/cla, and providing the
Corporation's legal name and jurisdiction, the representative's name
and title, and the initial schedule of Designated Contributors (names,
GitHub usernames, and email addresses). The signed record produced by
the signing service is the authoritative log of acceptance.

Alternatively, an authorized representative may accept by emailing a
completed copy of this Agreement to legal@vaelii.com, stating the
same information. Acceptance by email is effective upon Our written
confirmation; Our record of that exchange is then the authoritative
log of acceptance.

Designated Contributors also complete the individual cla-assistant
flow on their first pull request so the automated check passes. As
stated in the preamble, for Contributions made on the Corporation's
behalf this Agreement prevails over any ICLA signed by the individual.
