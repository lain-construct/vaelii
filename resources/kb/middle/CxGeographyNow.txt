;; CxGeographyNow — present-day political geography: which regions are countries, which
;; regions are parts of which, and which countries border which. Sees CxNow, so its
;; facts hold now and carry no temporal qualification. A human's whereabouts are stated
;; in a time context, (CxTimeFn CxGeographyNow (DatetimeFn …)), which CxTime places
;; below CxGeographyNow with no genlCx edge stated. CxTime orders the time contexts by
;; subintervalOf, so a day sees its month and a month its year, and two sibling months see
;; neither each other nor each other's whereabouts. A human relates to a region by
;; partOfRegion, as a region does: locatedIn relates two tangibles, and a country is
;; intangible.
;;
;; Ships as schema: the vocabulary and the rules below. The regions, borders and
;; partitions themselves are contingent facts and are stated by whoever reads the
;; geography.
;;
;; A region's parts are stated with partOfRegion and its divisions with
;; spatiallyPartitionedByType below, whose rules conclude partOfRegion and
;; regionDiscreteFrom, so the RCC-8 network reads both. bordersOn is CxSpace's open
;; neighbour relation. countryBordersOn is its spec typed to countries, and a border
;; between two countries is stated as countryBordersOn, so a closedExtentForArg grant over
;; an audited neighbour list reads every stated border between countries and closes
;; nothing above it.

(genlCx CxGeographyNow CxNow)
;; The starter loads CxRealWorld after the middle layer, so CxNow reaches CxUniverse only
;; once the load is done.  The direct edge to CxUniverse, the middle spindle's head, lets
;; the declarations below meet CxCore's rules while the layer loads.
(genlCx CxGeographyNow CxUniverse)

(comment country
         "A country's territory: the region a sovereign state governs. A region occupies space without being made of anything, so a country is spatiotemporal and intangible.")
(genl country spatiotemporal)
(genl country intangible)

(comment countryBordersOn
         "(countryBordersOn ?country1 ?country2) means that the countries ?country1 and ?country2 are neighbours across a shared border. A spec of bordersOn typed to countries, stated directly, so a closedExtentForArg grant on it reads every stated border between countries and leaves bordersOn open.")
(binary_predicate countryBordersOn)
(set/monotonic (instance_relation_predicate countryBordersOn))
(arg countryBordersOn 1 country)
(arg countryBordersOn 2 country)
(genl countryBordersOn bordersOn)
(typedSpec bordersOn countryBordersOn)

;; ---- a whole region and the type whose instances partition it -------------------
;; The region counterpart of CxCore's partitionedByType.  partitionedByType relates a type
;; to a classifier of its subtypes and reads membership; spatiallyPartitionedByType
;; relates a region individual to a type whose instances are its parts, and reads RCC-8.
;; A generator stamps two strict rules per fact: every instance of the classifier is
;; partOfRegion the whole, and two different instances are regionDiscreteFrom each other,
;; RCC-8's DR, so two parts may touch along a border.  The rules read the classifier's
;; memberships, so an instance stated after the partition is placed and separated as one
;; stated before it.
;;
;; It lives here rather than in CxSpace because its rules conclude RCC-8 predicates.  With
;; the RCC-8 prover registered, a fact on a predicate some rule names sends the settle
;; through the network, so a rule in CxSpace would put that cost on every KB that loads
;; CxSpace.  Here it falls on the KBs that load this theory.
(comment spatiallyPartitionedByType
         "(spatiallyPartitionedByType ?whole ?classifier) means that the instances of ?classifier partition the region ?whole: each instance is partOfRegion ?whole, no two instances share a part, and every part of ?whole lies within the union of the instances. e.g. (spatiallyPartitionedByType UnitedStates50AndDC us_state_or_dc) says that the fifty states and the District partition the region they make up. Two instances may touch along a border.")
(binary_predicate spatiallyPartitionedByType)
(arg spatiallyPartitionedByType 1 spatial)
(arg spatiallyPartitionedByType 2 unary_predicate)
(implies (spatiallyPartitionedByType ?whole ?classifier)
         (set/forwardRule (implies (?classifier ?part) (partOfRegion ?part ?whole))))
(implies (spatiallyPartitionedByType ?whole ?classifier)
         (set/forwardRule (implies (and (?classifier ?part1) (?classifier ?part2)
                                        (different ?part1 ?part2))
                                   (regionDiscreteFrom ?part1 ?part2))))
(implementationNote spatiallyPartitionedByType "No rule draws the coverage half: a region partOfRegion ?whole and regionDiscreteFrom every instance of ?classifier but one is partOfRegion the remaining instance, and a rule reading every instance but one needs engine support for ranging over a classifier's members, as partitionedByType's coverage does: https://github.com/vaelii/vaelii/issues/171")
(termsRelated partitionedByType spatiallyPartitionedByType)
(termsRelated spatiallyPartitionedByType partOfRegion regionDiscreteFrom)
