;; CxNow — the real world at the present time. Sees CxRealWorld, so it inherits the
;; whole shared ontology and excludes every fiction. CxWell does not see it: a context
;; opts in by placing itself under CxNow.
;;
;; A sentence stated here holds now and carries no temporal qualification. A context
;; about a time when what holds now need not have held is not placed below CxNow. A time
;; context, a (CxTimeFn ?base …) application, sees its ?base and the coarser time contexts
;; CxTime orders it below, so two sibling times see neither each other nor each other's
;; facts, and a time whose base is CxNow sees CxNow: a time when what holds now need not
;; have held takes another base.

(genlCx CxNow CxRealWorld)
