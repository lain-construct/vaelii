;; CxPerception — perception relations: an entity taking in a located thing through
;; sight. Sees the shared upper ontology via CxUniverse. CxWell does not see it: a
;; context opts in by placing itself under CxPerception.
;;
;; Arg 1 of every relation here names the perceiver and carries no arg declaration:
;; vaelii's `agent` names a registered koinii participant
;; (resources/kb/koinii/CxRegistry.txt), which is a membership mark for an
;; adjudication registry rather than a general type for an entity with agency, and
;; the upper ontology ships no such type, so no type below `thing` covers the
;; position (`ontology_test`'s `untyped-positions` roster, which `(arg … 1 thing)`
;; would convict: `starter_test`/`no-shipped-declaration-makes-an-argument-a-thing`).
;; `seeImage` and `watchVideo`'s second argument is the same: a thing of any kind, so
;; it carries no declaration either.

(genlCx CxPerception CxUniverse)

;; ---- the perception chain ------------------------------------------------
(comment perceives
         "(perceives ?perceiver ?perceived) means ?perceiver takes in ?perceived through some sense. The most general perception relation; sees, seeImage and watchVideo each specialize it.")
(binary_predicate perceives)
(instance_relation_predicate perceives)
(arg perceives 2 spatial)

(comment sees
         "(sees ?seer ?seen) means ?seer takes in ?seen through sight. Spec of perceives.")
(binary_predicate sees)
(instance_relation_predicate sees)
(arg sees 2 spatial)
(genl sees perceives)

(comment seeImage
         "(seeImage ?seer ?image) means ?seer takes in a static image through sight and perceives what it shows. Spec of sees; its second argument is the image itself rather than a located thing, since an image can depict something with no location of its own.")
(binary_predicate seeImage)
(instance_relation_predicate seeImage)
(genl seeImage sees)

(comment watchVideo
         "(watchVideo ?watcher ?video) means ?watcher takes in a video, a temporal sequence of images, through sight. Spec of sees.")
(binary_predicate watchVideo)
(instance_relation_predicate watchVideo)
(genl watchVideo sees)

;; ---- image_viewing: the ability, read as an event kind -------------------
;; CxLife's hasCapability takes an event kind as its second argument
;; (resources/kb/upper/CxLife.txt): (hasCapability ?x ?eventKind) means ?x can be the
;; doer of an event of that kind. image_viewing pairs with seeImage the way flying
;; pairs with flies: the relation states who is looking, the event kind states the
;; ability to be its doer. Taking in an image changes nothing about what it depicts,
;; so image_viewing is a kind of acausal_event, CxAbstract's intersection of acausal
;; and event, whose own comment names an observation as an example.
(comment image_viewing
         "An event of taking in a static image through sight and perceiving what it shows. The event-kind counterpart of seeImage: (hasCapability ?x image_viewing) means ?x can be the doer of one.")
(genl image_viewing acausal_event)
