;; CxUniverse — the collector of the upper spindle.  Sees every upper member
;; (CxAbstract, CxOrganism, CxSociety, CxLife, CxMeasure, CxSpace, CxTime),
;; so cross-member disjointness assertions that require both sides visible
;; live here rather than in CxCore (which cannot see the members) or in one
;; member (which cannot see the other).  A claim whose terms one member sees belongs
;; to that member.

;; --- cross-member type placement ---
;; initially is CxTime's and fluent is CxAbstract's; neither member sees the other.
(genl initially fluent)

;; The seven biology properties are CxLife's; animal is CxOrganism's, which CxLife
;; does not see.  Each is placed under the kind its arg 1 names, and the three over
;; biological and organism (CxCore's) are stated here beside the four over animal so
;; the seven read as one decision.  No kind is placed under any of them.
(genl alive biological)
(genl dead biological)
(genl mortal organism)
(genl asleep animal)
(genl awake animal)
(genl breathes_air animal)
(genl warm_blooded animal)

;; --- cross-member disjointness (upper spindle) ---
;; None is stated today.  organization and animal, the pair that stood here, are
;; separated by (partition thing tangible intangible) in CxCore: organization is
;; intangible and animal is tangible.

;; --- cross-member related clusters (upper spindle) ---
;; time_interval is CxAbstract's; Duration, the dimension a stretch of time is
;; measured in, is CxMeasure's.
(termsRelated time_interval Duration)

;; --- travelling / flying: event kinds an ability names ------------------------
;; CxLife's hasCapability and capabilityType take an event kind as their second argument:
;; (hasCapability Tweety flying) says Tweety can be the doer of a flying event.  The event
;; kinds are stated in the collector: they relate CxLife's abilities to CxAbstract's causal_event,
;; and only the collector sees both.
(comment travelling "A causal event of getting from one place to another under the doer's own power. flying is a kind of travelling; another way of travelling is stated as its own event kind below travelling.")
(genl travelling causal_event)

(comment flying "A travelling event through the air under the doer's own power. A kind of travelling, so whatever can fly can travel without that being stated twice.")
(genl flying travelling)
