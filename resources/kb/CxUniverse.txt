;; member (which cannot see the other).  A claim whose terms one member sees belongs
;; to that member.

;; --- cross-member disjointness (upper spindle) ---
;; organization and animal are separated by (partition thing tangible intangible) in
;; CxCore: organization is intangible and animal is tangible.  animal is also disjoint
;; from four of CxAbstract's made kinds: an animal is never a building, a piece of
;; clothing, a furniture or a machine.
(set/monotonic (disjoint animal building))
(set/monotonic (disjoint animal clothing))
(set/monotonic (disjoint animal furniture))
(set/monotonic (disjoint animal machine))
;; A bonsai or a grafted apple tree is a made plant, and a rock is natural and no plant.
;; The fact derives plant's orthogonals with made and with natural (origin_type is
;; CxAbstract's, plant CxOrganism's).
(typeOrthogonal origin_type plant)
;; Mammals and birds are warm-blooded, so a warm-blooded animal is a vertebrate by default
;; (warm_blooded is CxLife's, vertebrate CxOrganism's).
(genl warm_blooded vertebrate)
;; A cloned sheep is a made vertebrate, and a lab-bred fruit fly a made invertebrate (made is
;; CxAbstract's, vertebrate and invertebrate CxOrganism's).
(orthogonal made vertebrate)
(orthogonal made invertebrate)
;; A water level or a tap's rate is a quantity, and a quantity causes nothing; what changes
;; it does (quantity is CxMeasure's, acausal CxAbstract's).
(genl quantity acausal)
;; animal and vehicle are disjoint at the default strength rather than monotonic: a
;; fiction below CxWell may except the default and hold a term that is both. CxRealWorld
;; restates the same separation monotonic, so the real world can never hold the overlap.
(disjoint animal vehicle)

;; --- a cause starts no later than its effect ------------------------------------
;; causes is CxAbstract's and StartFn is CxTime's, so only the collector sees both.  The
;; rule concludes a point relation, which the point network reads: with the point reasoner
;; registered, a stated order that puts an effect's start before its cause's is a
;; qualitative inconsistency.  A cause may go on after its effect starts.
(set/forwardRule
 (implies (and (causes ?cause ?effect))
          (instantNotAfter (StartFn ?cause) (StartFn ?effect))))

;; --- travelling / flying: event kinds an ability names ------------------------
;; CxLife's hasCapability and capabilityType take an event kind as their second argument:
;; (hasCapability Tweety flying) says Tweety can be the doer of a flying event.  The event
;; kinds are stated in the collector: they relate CxLife's abilities to CxAbstract's causal_event,
;; and only the collector sees both.
(comment travelling "A causal event of getting from one place to another under the doer's own power. flying is a kind of travelling; another way of travelling is stated as its own event kind below travelling.")
(genl travelling causal_event)

(comment flying "A travelling event through the air under the doer's own power. A kind of travelling, so whatever can fly can travel without that being stated twice.")
(genl flying travelling)
