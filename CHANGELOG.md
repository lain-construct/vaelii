# Changelog

# The first 12 days of Vaelii

## 2026-07-30

- **A declaration re-checks the exceptions it moves.** `(symmetric P)`,
  `(transitive P)`, `(inverse P Q)` and the `argPreserving` forms change
  what may be concluded with no fact arriving; withdrawal takes back what
  each licensed, and `(functional P)` sweeps the extent when it lands.
- **An equality restates a fact for each reader, not once for the KB.** A
  merge holds where it is visible, so every context below one reads the
  fact once, under the name it elects rather than one an edge it cannot
  see chose.
- **An equality is a re-check trigger**, since a merge retires a spelling
  without removing the fact and rewrites the question an `exceptWhen`, an
  `unknown` or a census asks — down to the bindings a firing stored.
- **A change feed.** The region a settle already computes is handed to a
  listener instead of discarded: watch belief rather than poll for it.
- **English in.** Read a sentence and produce candidates a person still
  has to accept. The path deliberately stops short of asserting.
- A qualitative relation two microtheories entail *together* fires a
  forward rule, a believed negative one reaches the wiring a positive one
  does, and "some context" means the union of what the readers answer.
- Over a cyclic hierarchy three readers of one question give one answer,
  and a settle repairs the context ranking after reconciling belief as
  well as before it.
- `deprecated?` takes a context like the class reads beside it, and the
  LLM seam judges the knowledge base's own conclusions from outside.

## 2026-07-29

- **One front door for backward chaining.** The four backward paths were
  measured against each other and documented side by side, then
  consolidated: two chainers behind a single entry point with one dial.
  A proof of an ephemeral answer now reads the way `why` does.
- **Ordering became a policy.** The goal frontier's order is chosen
  rather than fixed, measured on time-to-first-answer, with
  `:ground-first` as the default because the measurement said so.
- The goal-stack chainer can be driven one solution at a time, and level
  7 streams its search instead of finishing it first.
- **Foreign formats arrive as a plugin.** A format the engine reads and
  does not write is resolved from the classpath, so a reader can ship
  and retire without touching the engine.
- A constraint declaration may name a second sentex it must not weigh.
- A depth bound has no default, because there is no defensible one.

## 2026-07-28

- **`lein gate`.** Lint, the suite, and the scaling claims, which are
  measured and failed on rather than asserted.
- **The columnar index is written once and mapped back.** The image
  works; the doc says plainly which of the two things it was built to
  prove did not.
- Backend names read `<records>-<index>`, all seven of them, so the two
  axes are visible in the name.
- A literal's matches are remembered and retired on a clock; branches
  that converge on the same rule expansion share it.
- A third backward chainer whose state is a frontier rather than a path.
- The naming invariants belong to the knowledge base, and the bulk door
  counts what it skips.
- Five checks added for costs that grow with what they must not.

## 2026-07-27

- **Aggregation.** A count is a query operator, and a firing that rests
  on one is maintained like any other. Documented with every example run
  rather than written, and gated by a permutation test.
- A census counts distinctness through the representative the asking
  context elects, and a `genlContext` edge re-checks the rule a rising
  count licenses a *first* firing for.
- **`argIsa` entails as well as constrains**, behind a toggle, with the
  retroactive direction folded through the same read.
- A definitional clash names a second sentex, which makes it a nogood,
  and the sweep that arbitrates one asks the taxonomy rather than a fixed
  set of functors, so a disjoint metatype is arbitrated where it is
  exposed. The standing disjointness question can be asked on demand.
- The qualitative network lives on the knowledge base and warm-starts off
  its own previous answer; a rule re-joins only the pairs that moved, and
  a violation ledger is a claim about a knowledge base rather than about
  the network two share.
- The browser draws the shape of a term, composes English from the
  knowledge base's own comments at three densities, renders a reified
  term as what it denotes, and gained `lein browser`: a REPL with the
  browser in it.
- Loading OpenCyc went from 378s to 277s against five named costs.

## 2026-07-26

- **Contexts got a vantage.** Every taxonomy supporter records the
  context it asserts from, so reads are filtered by what the asking
  context can see: disjointness convicts only on grounds visible to the
  asserting context, the matching fan-out walks only visible edges, and
  settle exposes the clash that no single writer could see.
- A firing names the `genl` edges it subsumed through, so belief and
  strength run through them like any other antecedent. Checked against
  all 24 orderings of a firing that subsumes.
- **Records and index became two independent choices** rather than one
  backend selection.
- **An overlay backend**: a private writable fork over a shared
  read-only base, whose index projection is the merged view.
- A knowledge base is readable before it has finished loading.
- The suite runs on every backend from one script.

## 2026-07-25

- **OpenCyc, read and republished.** Every constant is given back its
  role, a computed microtheory is one context wherever it is named, and
  the result is published in the engine's own format at 1.1M sentexes.
- **An export format no rename can break**, with `xz`, an importer for
  the engine's own dialect, and an oracle that compares two knowledge
  bases. A dump lands every record at the handle it left with.
- **Qualitative spatial and temporal reasoning**: RCC-8 over a constraint
  network that knows nothing about a knowledge base, then three more
  spatial algebras, Allen intervals, durations, and instants, all behind
  one glue.
- **One gap written in two units is one constraint.** `1.1 Hour` arrives
  as 3960.0000000000005 seconds where `66 Minute` arrives as 3960, so
  both snap to the tolerance grid a stored `length` snaps to, and the
  closure judges crossed bounds and lost time to the same epsilon. A unit
  given two conversion factors is its own base rather than converting by
  whichever was indexed first.
- **A knowledge-base catalog**: what a process can load, what it is on,
  and a browser that switches between them, plus a generator that builds
  a knowledge base from numbers.
- `inherit`: whether a claim about two kinds reaches their subkinds,
  declared rather than assumed. Definitional checks reach every term, not
  only individuals; `argGenl` constrains one level up; arity is held by
  the sentence and by the declarations alike.

## 2026-07-24

- **The scale program opened.** A build plan to reach 100M sentexes and
  10M rules, then measurement first: the truth-maintenance wall, a
  posting-encoding bake-off validated on a real corpus, and a rule audit.
- **Three dense representations, each measured.** A `:memory-dense` index
  backend on integer postings, a `:memory-columnar` native int-token trie
  with CSR compaction and int-keyed roots for 3.18x on the whole index,
  and a bitmapped truth-maintenance network behind a protocol, with the
  persistent map kept as one implementation.
- **The disk side got dense too**: positional record reads with a
  hot-record LRU, a positional frame codec, and tokenized record bodies
  over a durable dictionary. Guarded by a backend parity gate.
- Recursive forward chaining went from O(n³) to O(n log n) on a
  redundant-justification fast path.
- A term roster enumerates the vocabulary in O(terms), not O(sentexes).
- **The web browser was hardened**: escape by default, guard the parse,
  bind loopback, refuse cross-origin writes.
- **A pluggable LLM that proposes edits and never applies them**, with a
  provider seam, a local Ollama backend, and proposals scoped to a
  selection and diffed back into the editor.

## 2026-07-23

- **A performance review, and its findings fixed.** The disk log records
  the operation rather than the grown value, killing an O(N²) write
  amplification; settle maintains a coincidence set instead of rescanning
  negations; the exception re-check narrows to the moved cone; region
  fixpoints became semi-naive worklists; record-store compaction is
  copy-on-write.
- **Symbolic equational reasoning**: compound equality over reifiable
  terms, a pure oriented term-rewriting engine, full Knuth-Bendix
  orientation, order-independent normalization, and non-confluent
  conflicts surfaced rather than silently resolved. `rewriteOf` extended
  over predicates and types, carrying a rule's guards with it.
- **An operational surface**: an EDN-over-HTTP daemon, a command-line
  driver, a thin client, and a browser that can attach to a daemon.
- The public API closed the holes the browser was tracking, so the
  browser reaches zero implementation namespaces.
- Existential rule heads with deterministic skolemization; an occurs
  check in unification; closures answered on demand rather than
  materialized.
- The record split into an atomic and a rule shape, with interned
  sentence symbols; a bulk-load fast path, and one settle per batch.
- The browser gained themes, automatic dark mode, drag-select bulk
  editing, and a regex term-finder.

## 2026-07-22

- **Negation as failure**, at the top level and in rule antecedents,
  with block, sweep and revive, and stratification to keep it sound.
- **Resource-bounded, anytime inference** with qualitative prover cost
  tiers, and a cost-ordered forward-chain join. `ask-within` normalizes
  its goal through the step every read entry shares, so a goal naming a
  reifiable NAT is answered rather than coming back empty.
- **Reification of non-atomic terms**: mint, dedup, expand and rename.
- **Structural subterm indexing.** Parenthesis-marked encoding, planner
  descent into it, an oracle to prove it equivalent, then on by default.
- **The storage seam.** The trie, roots and index logic moved onto one
  key-value backend protocol, then an in-memory backend, then the
  on-disk substrate: files, lock, durability, record store, index store.
- The index benchmark harness, and the measured finding of how much to
  index and where the knee is.
- A per-handle provenance side map, open for application fields.

## 2026-07-21

- **`exceptWhen` canonicalized into the record**, blocking excepted
  conclusions, with only the firings a change can reach re-checked. An
  exception's query is reified the way a fact is, so one naming a
  reifiable NAT asks about the constant the fact was stored under.
- **Equality landed**: the closure, the `different` prover, a
  specification suite, and wiring into assert.
- **Stratification is checked on edge change**, and cycles through
  negation are rejected.
- The engine split out of one namespace into five, with `core` keeping
  the API, and the knowledge base restructured into a layered tree loaded
  on start.
- `assumptionRules` with persistent solve and labeling contexts, proven
  on a sudoku, with an assumption rule's exception honored at grounding.
- **Retrieval got sharper**: argument roots for leading-variable
  patterns, multi-column narrowing across functor and arguments,
  predicate subsumption in matching, set-algebra retrieval for the
  hierarchical query, and an opt-in incremental forward-chain matcher.
- Truth-maintenance mutations are atomic, and retracting an unknown
  handle is a no-op. Lint arrived: clj-kondo, cljfmt, and `lein lint`.

## 2026-07-20

- **Canonical rule form**: canonical variables with a varmap, literal
  order, and comparison direction, so rules alike up to renaming share
  one handle.
- **The eight-level lookup-to-query stack**, lazy throughout, with a page
  in the browser that shows which level answered. Every level answers the
  question `ask` asks — 6 and 7 normalize the goal, 5 folds its matches
  on the goal seen through the bindings, 3 scopes a retired spelling to
  its reader — and it is monotonic but for the two joints it names.
- **Order independence and locality were pinned as invariants**, and the
  JTMS relabelling became region-local. The cached closures follow belief,
  are maintained incrementally, and key on content: a clash report's sides
  follow the order of its sentence, not the order the two were typed in.
- **The answer-set layer** wired to the edge-solver seam, guarded in both
  directions, with a settled tie classified and a labeling materialized
  as a context.
- The defeasible layer was made sound: strength propagation and
  derived-conclusion checks, with six confirmed soundness bugs pinned as
  failing tests first.
- `exceptWhen` began: the design, a failing suite that specifies it, the
  exception re-check index, and blocked justifications reaching the sweep.
- Everything but `core` moved under `vaelii.impl.*`; `!` was reserved for
  irreversible operations; the context relation and context names took
  their current spelling.
- Tests became net-neutral, with generated temporaries and teardown
  guards, and a second concurrent run fails fast rather than corrupting
  the first.

## 2026-07-19

The first day: a contextualized common-sense knowledge base with a trie
index, inference and truth maintenance.

- **Sentexes**, a sentence plus the context it holds in, stored as
  records behind protocols with nippy serialization.
- **Forward chaining with dependency-directed retraction**, and a
  backward chainer.
- **Rules are sentexes too**, so they get a handle, support and
  retraction for free, with built-in transitivity for types and
  contexts.
- **A non-monotonic truth-maintenance system**: strengths, soft
  prioritized contradictions, and a solver seam.
- An inverted term index, directed rules, disjointness, well-formedness
  checks, and a pluggable prover query engine.
- Structural connectives canonicalize into the record, and rules dedup up
  to variable renaming.
- Evaluable arithmetic and argument-type inference.
- A web browser over the whole thing, linking sentexes, subterms and
  handles, over a starter ontology with every term documented.
